Jiaxuan Lin

Email / CV / GitHub / Photography

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Science and Technology of China, where I work with Prof. Yanyong Zhang and Prof. Jianmin Ji. I am also a visiting research intern at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. David Hsu.

My research interests lie in robot learning for dexterous manipulation. I am particularly interested in learning from human videos and physical interaction, with reinforcement learning and vision-language-action (VLA) models.

Outside research, I enjoy photography, mostly cityscapes and light.
